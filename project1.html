Saarthi — Navigate Every City Like a Local

📌 Overview

Saarthi (meaning: Companion) is a smart web-based travel assistance platform designed to help travelers find reliable information in an unfamiliar city. Whether it is finding a safe hotel, a good restaurant, or an emergency hospital, Saarthi works as a digital companion to simplify travel.

🚀 Problem & Solution

Most travel platforms focus on paid promotions instead of quality recommendations. Saarthi solves this problem by providing recommendations based purely on genuine ratings. This ensures users always get trustworthy and unbiased results.

⚙️ Technical Workflow

• Dynamic Location Selection (Country → State → City) using JavaScript

• Top 5 curated recommendations instead of hundreds of confusing results

• Simple and fast interface for quick access to information
