Rakshak — Smart Farm Protection & Automation System

📌 Overview

Rakshak is a smart farm protection and automation system designed to help farmers protect their crops from multiple threats. It works like an automated monitoring system that detects animals, fire, and environmental conditions in real time. The system combines different sensors and modules to ensure crop safety and efficient irrigation without constant human supervision.

🚀 Problem & Solution

Farmers face multiple challenges such as animal attacks, fire hazards, and inefficient irrigation. Traditional methods require constant monitoring and manual effort. Rakshak solves this by automating detection and response using sensors. It provides real-time alerts and automatic actions, reducing human effort and improving farm safety.

⚙️ Working

Rakshak uses multiple sensors connected with Arduino Uno and ESP8266.

• Flame Sensor detects fire and sends alerts to the farmer’s phone.

• IR Sensors are placed around the farm boundary to detect animals entering from ground level and trigger sound alerts.

• Ultrasonic Sensor (HC-SR04) rotates to monitor all directions and detects birds or flying animals, activating deterrent signals.

• Soil Moisture Sensor monitors soil humidity and automatically activates irrigation when moisture drops below a certain level.

• Gyroscope Sensor measures wind speed and direction and sends data to the application.

All system data and alerts are intended to be monitored through a mobile application.
